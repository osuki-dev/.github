We build tools for working with coding agents, connecting to your own computer, and bringing diagrams to native apps.

### Muqun App

**Your agents, anywhere.** Follow coding agents, use the real terminal, and handle approvals from your phone. Connect to your own machines and make Muqun yours with custom themes. Available for iOS and Android.

### Muqun Gateway

A self-hosted bridge to terminals and coding agents on your machine. No Muqun account or hosted relay. Runs on macOS and Linux.

[<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/osuki-dev/.github/main/assets/muqun-gateway.gif" alt="Connection schematic: Muqun App connects over HTTPS and SSE to Gateway on your computer, which connects to terminals and agents" width="100%">](https://github.com/osuki-dev/muqun-gateway)

[Installation & setup](https://github.com/osuki-dev/muqun-gateway#install) · [Explore the source](https://github.com/osuki-dev/muqun-gateway)

### Skia Diagrams

Mermaid parsing and layout with native React Native Skia rendering. No DOM or WebView. Bring flowcharts, sequence diagrams, mindmaps and more into your app.
